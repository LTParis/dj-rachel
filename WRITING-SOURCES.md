## Search coverage and practical limits

Searched Rachel Lynch, Rachel R Lynch, and DJ Rachel with article/byline terms across DJ Blog, Promo Only, DJNTV / Disc Jockey News, DJX / DJ LIFE, Digital DJ Tips, My Wedding Songs, and the wider web. Checked archive/category results, the My Wedding Songs author archive, and adjacent DJX posts to distinguish authorship from mentions.

DJ Blog appears connected with Promo Only: multiple contributors describe writing there for Promo Only. The cards use the verifiable site name DJ Blog rather than presenting the same pieces as additional Promo Only articles. DJ Blog has intermittent 502/503 responses; indexed publisher content supported verification, but the archive cannot be guaranteed complete.

DJNTV and Disc Jockey News searches surfaced Rachel’s Summer Shortz / VirtualDJ video tutorials and a members-only Social Media and the Mobile DJ session (https://djntvtraining.com/). These are educational video/session contributions, not confirmed authored written articles. DJNTV pages/API could not be reliably retrieved, so no claim is made that Rachel has no other written contributions there. They were not mislabelled as articles in the Writing section.

Excluded interviews, event listings, announcements, and articles by others that merely mention Rachel, including the DJX CPR announcements credited to Mark Mancino and the Ethics of Faking a DJ Set essay credited to Mike Garrett. Also excluded unrelated authors with the same name. This is a verified inventory of what could be found, not a guarantee that every historical or unindexed article has been recovered.
