If you think a wedding DJ is just music and a few announcements, you've only seen the last 12 hours of the job. A wedding DJ isn't an hourly service. Here's why.

## You're investing in an outcome

What you're hiring is talent, delivered through months of planning, preparation, and personalization, long before I ever show up. Most of the work happens before your wedding day:

- Tailoring your timeline
- Coordinating with your other vendors
- Prepping your music and making specialty edits
- Rehearsing names and spotlight moments
- Visiting the venue and meeting with you
- Testing equipment
- Designing lighting and sound to complement your space
- Packing hundreds of pounds of gear
- Setting up thousands of dollars in tech and backup systems

And then comes the 12-hour event day, where I deliver exactly what you wanted, elevated and creative.

## The details no one planned for

It's handling surprises and stressful unknowns so smoothly that they feel like they were always meant to happen.

That takes intuition, empathy, and real experience. You're not renting speakers. You're paying for the talent to get the things that matter most to you right, in a way that feels genuine.

## There are no redos

You're paying for someone who cares about your day as much as you do, and who delivers on every promise. I know there's no second chance at this day. I'm thinking about that every minute, right up until I head home and you're still celebrating with the people you love most. ❤️
