Controller? Check. Massive music library? Check. Pro sound, fancy lighting, 500 business cards? Check, check, check.

Now you can call yourself a DJ. Or can you?

Gear and know-how are just the entry fee. When I started, I had no idea how many other jobs came with this one. After all these years, my definition of "DJ" has flipped completely. Here are the hats I never saw coming.

## The Salesperson

I hate sales. Or so I thought. I avoided sales and math at every job I ever had, and I figured DJing was my escape route. Wrong.

Getting the gig matters as much as having the gear. The biggest shift in my business came when I stopped selling equipment and years of experience and started selling me. Without value, everyone shops on price. Show people how having you behind the decks changes the entire experience.

Charm, wit, kindness, and dependability beat the watts on your sub. Honestly, nobody cares about the watts except other DJs.

## The Marketing Manager

Back in my early days, it was the Yellow Pages, promoters, and business cards. (Yes, I'm dating myself.) Now you need to know where your clients hang out and show up there. Word of mouth is still king, but you have to make your own flyers, videos, and posts to be seen. The good news is that apps like Canva, Clips, iMovie, Spark Post, and Grammarly make it painless.

I'm not here to tell you how to market. I'm here to tell you why you must.

## The R&D Department

"DJs just play music, right?" Sure, if you ignore where music comes from. Songs now blow up because of a viral dance, a TV moment, or a controversial performance. Your guests already know what they love, and you need to know it too.

So do your homework (better than you did in middle school). Set aside time each week for pop culture and trending music. It keeps you fresh. Trust me.

## The IT Department

DMX programming, EQ settings, firmware updates, wireless gear, and troubleshooting when things go wrong (they will). You're the tech department. Roll up your sleeves and get your hands dirty, because technology isn't slowing down.

## The Takeaway

If you're just starting out, get ready to grow in ways you never planned. We're so much more than "just a DJ."
