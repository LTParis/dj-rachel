Our industry runs on relationships. With people. With energy. With music.

Every time I walk into a room, I'm facing a crowd with different backgrounds, ages, cultures, and tastes, and each person has their own history with music. My job is to bring all of them together in a way that actually means something.

Here's the truth: DJing isn't really about the music. It's about people. It's about empathy. The best DJs I know have a ton of it.

## Reading a room is more than a full dance floor

"Is the dance floor full?" is a surface-level question. Empathy goes deeper. It means stepping outside yourself to understand what someone else is feeling, experiencing, or needing in that moment.

It's knowing why someone requests a song, why a certain track gets a reaction, and how people want to feel. So I:

- Pay attention
- Listen
- Observe
- Adjust

My personal taste doesn't outrank the experience unfolding in front of me. Empathy means putting my ego aside.

## It goes way beyond the music

Empathy is how you:

- Talk with a nervous couple and put them at ease
- Know when the mic needs more energy and when to get out of the way
- Spot the moment that deserves the spotlight, and recognize when your presence would only distract from it

Every person in that room is experiencing the same event differently. Seeing that is the job.

Reading a room is an exercise in empathy. Everything else is just music.
