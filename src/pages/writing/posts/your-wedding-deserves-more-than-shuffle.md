If you can find funds to allocate to someone who is professionally dedicated to showing you and your guests a good time, it's worth it. A great DJ cannot be replaced by a Spotify playlist, and here's the proof.

Using a Spotify playlist is like serving your guests a frozen dinner at your wedding. Will they eat it? Sure. But it won't be as satisfying or memorable.

When you hire a great DJ, you get far more than "just music and a few announcements." I get the obvious details right and the unspoken nuances, too: the little things you never thought to mention, or never thought of at all.

Or maybe you did mention them. Picture this: during our planning meeting, you casually share that your dad used to make breakfast every Sunday morning with a particular artist playing in the kitchen. It isn't a song request. You don't even expect me to remember. It's just a little story you tell while talking about your family.

But I remember. And during dinner, while your dad is sitting across the table, that familiar song begins to play.

I'm always listening, looking for meaningful ways to tie your memories to music. That's how I create opportunities to celebrate in a special way a general playlist can't. You'll never know what you missed without someone professionally dedicated to creating fun, memorable moments for you and the people you've invited to celebrate with you.

I'm not saying your party will be a complete disaster without a DJ. I'm saying you and your guests are more likely to enjoy great entertainment and a great outcome with an awesome DJ than from the money spent on centerpieces, linens, flowers, cake, or a passive playlist. The entertainment and the experience are what people remember and appreciate.

No, you don't need a DJ. But if you want your celebration to look and feel like a real celebration, I'd consider it.
